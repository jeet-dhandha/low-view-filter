Privacy Policy

Low-View Filter for YouTube — last updated 13 November 2026

Low-View Filter for YouTube ("the extension") hides YouTube search and feed results that fall below a view count or upload date chosen by the user.

What the extension stores

Your filter settings — view floor, upload cut-off, rule mode and the feature toggles — are saved with chrome.storage.sync. That storage belongs to Chrome and is tied to your Chrome profile. If you have Chrome Sync turned on, Chrome may sync those settings between your own devices; that sync is performed by Chrome, not by us.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.
  • It does not read your browsing history, cookies, accounts or identity.
  • It does not make network requests of its own, and it contains no analytics, telemetry or advertising code.
  • It does not use remote code. All code ships inside the extension package.
  • It does not modify YouTube's servers or your account in any way.

What the extension reads

On youtube.com pages only, the extension reads the text already displayed on result cards — the view count and upload date — to decide whether a card matches your rule. That reading happens in your browser and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Third parties

No third parties receive data from this extension.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension removes its stored settings from that Chrome profile. You can also reset them at any time with the Reset button in the extension popup.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub or use the contact address on the Chrome Web Store item page.

Low-View Filter for YouTube is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
